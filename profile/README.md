<h1 align="center">BiznesIQ</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Small firms do not need reports. They need the work done.</strong></p>

BiznesIQ turns the enquiry into a quote, the quote into a scheduled job, and the finished job into an invoice that actually goes out. It drafts each step from what already exists in your inbox, and a person approves it. BiznesIQ is built on sovereign open source, on infrastructure you control.

## What it does

- **Quotes from a conversation.** The enquiry in your inbox becomes a draft quote.
- **Jobs and scheduling.** The accepted quote becomes a scheduled job with the right people on it.
- **Invoices that go out.** The finished job becomes an invoice, not a reminder to write one.
- **Runs where you decide.** Your server, your data, and you pay for value, not licences.
